## Key question 1: What paint/coating does Funktion-One use (brand, chemistry), and is it sprayed or rolled?

### Takeaway
Funktion-One currently runs a two-tier finish system: (a) the "classic Funktion-One violet" (and other RAL colours) which the manufacturer itself describes as "water based finishes", and (b) a black "ultra-durable / tough PU coating" which Funktion-One's Evolution brochure and the 2018 Vero user guide name explicitly as a **polyurea** coating, offered as the "highly durable and weather resistant" option. No manufacturer source names the paint brand or the application method; the long-standing community claim (2007 onward) is that the violet paint is an OEM version of **Warnex** (Warnecke & Böhm) water-based textured speaker paint, and that the texture is obtained by low-pressure spray "spatter" over a smooth coat.

### Inferences
- Funktion-One's "PU coating" on product pages and the "polyurea coating" in its Evolution brochure and Vero user guide almost certainly refer to the same black tough finish (same adjectives "highly durable", "weather resistant"); Funktion-One never expands the abbreviation on product pages, so "PU = polyurea" is an inference. Note that Martin Audio uses "PU" for polyurethane, so readers should not assume PU means polyurethane in the F1 context.
- Because the F132/V132 pages contrast "black PU coating" with "RALcode water based finishes", the standard violet and all RAL colours are water-based paint, while the black tough option is a different (polyurea) chemistry. This is consistent with the dealer-sold "Funktion One Water-Based Paint" tins and with the 2007 Warnex-OEM claim; the 2009 "epoxy" forum claim is the outlier.
- The brand of the water-based paint (Warnex OEM) and spray application are plausible but rest entirely on 2007-era forum posts; no manufacturer confirmation found.

### Gaps
- No manufacturer source names the paint supplier, the number of coats, film thickness, or whether application is spray or roller.
- No source identifies who applies the polyurea coating (in-house vs. contractor) or the polyurea product used.
- Could not verify the "Raptor liner" claim (search budget exhausted before a follow-up search).

### Takeaway
Funktion-One cabinets are birch plywood (18 mm on bass enclosures, 15 mm on the original Vero elements) cut in the company's own carpentry shop at Beare Green, Dorking; no public source describes sanding, sealing, priming or edge treatment.

## Key question 6: How does Funktion-One's approach compare with UK peers (Turbosound, Martin Audio, Void), and what is the historical thread?

### Takeaway
Funktion-One's water-based textured paint descends directly from Turbosound practice (Turbosound documented Warnecke & Böhm water-based paint on its Aspect series, applied without primer), while its newer black "PU"/polyurea option mirrors the industry shift seen at Void (TourCoat textured polyurea on 18 mm birch), Martin Audio ("textured black PU coating", polyurethane on weatherised AM series) and Turbosound (TourTough on Flashline). Funktion-One is unusual in keeping the water-based violet finish as its default brand identity rather than moving wholesale to polyurea.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The Warnecke & Böhm water-based paint documented by Turbosound in the Andrews/Newsham era is the most plausible origin of the Funktion-One violet finish (community says "OEM Warnex"); the 2007 "Dead Violet" part name and dealer-sold water-based tins fit this lineage.
- Across UK peers, "durable/weather-resistant" now means a sprayed polyurea or polyurethane textured coating; Funktion-One's "black PU coating" is its equivalent of Void's TourCoat / Turbosound's TourTough, but Funktion-One retains the water-based violet as the default, likely for brand identity (the violet is referenced in every product listing).
