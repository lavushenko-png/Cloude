# Cabinet finishes and coatings used by major pro-audio loudspeaker manufacturers (brand-by-brand)

Method note for the report writer: this session's network allowed WebSearch but blocked direct page fetches of every manufacturer domain (l-acoustics.com, dbaudio.com, docs.meyersound.com, harman.helpjuice.com, martin-audio.com, danleysoundlabs.com, kv2audio.com, codaaudio.com, fulcrum-acoustic.com, voidacoustics.com, fohhn.com, design.eaw.com, procom-me.com, goknight.com, tpimagazine.com). All quotations below are therefore taken from the search engine's excerpts of the cited documents, not from a full read of the PDFs. The search budget was exhausted before NEXO, Outline, Kling & Freitag, Clair Brothers and Alcons could be probed a second time; those brands are thin and flagged as gaps. "Mfr" = manufacturer's own document; "Retailer"/"Press"/"Forum" = secondary.

## Key question 1: Which specific coating chemistries do the major brands use? (brand-by-brand register)

### Takeaway
Two chemistries dominate the current (2015–2026) high end: (a) spray polyurea (d&b "PCP", Adamson IS-Series, Coda, KV2, Danley, RCF TT-series, dBTechnologies VIO, Martin Audio CDD-WR, Turbosound Flashline "TourTough") and (b) two-component polyurethane textured paint (L-Acoustics 2K PU base + water-based 1K PU topcoat, EAW "roadcoat" textured PU, Martin Audio TORUS/legacy AM "textured PU paint", HK Audio LINEAR 9/COSMO "PU coating"). Water-based acrylic texture (Warnex) is documented only at HK Audio LINEAR 7; Meyer Sound, JBL ("DuraFlex") and Electro-Voice ("EVCoat") publish trade names or "primer + topcoat" descriptions without disclosing chemistry.

## Key question 5: What do manufacturers sell or recommend for touch-up/repair?

### Takeaway
Official repair products are rare: JBL once sold a DuraFlex touch-up kit (P/N 30052) but a newer Harman FAQ says kits are no longer supplied and points to truck-bed-liner products; EAW publishes a repaint note (water-based semi-gloss Sherwin-Williams SW6258) and warns textured roadcoat is hard to match; d&b routes repairs to service partners. No L-Acoustics, Meyer, Adamson, Martin Audio, Coda, KV2 or RCF touch-up product was found.

### Cited Findings
- JBL: "a touch-up kit (Part Number 30052) is available from JBL Professional Parts or your in-country distributor" — [Harman: DuraFlex repair kit](https://harman.helpjuice.com/general-jbl-inquiries/duraflex-repair-kit); contradicting FAQ: "we no longer supply touch-up paint kits for our products", suggesting "truck bed liner paint, protective finish, rough coating, tough liner paint" — [Harman: DuraFlex alternatives](https://harman.helpjuice.com/general-jbl-inquiries/duraflex-alternatives)

## Key question 6: Do manufacturers offer custom RAL colours, and are they painted over the texture coat or tinted into it?
