# Indonesia sourcing guide: paints, coatings and applicators for a dark-grey matte textured pro-audio cabinet finish

Research date: 2026-11-19. All IDR prices are marketplace snapshots as seen in search-result excerpts on that date (listing pages themselves could not be opened from this session: tokopedia.com, shopee.co.id, biggo.id, thomann.de, penn-elcom.com, bluearan.co.uk, acrytech.com, jotun.com, propanraya.com, avianbrands.com, indana.co.id and the Indonesian polyurea contractor sites were all outside the session's allowed network, so figures come from search snippets and should be re-verified on the live page before purchase). USD conversions use an approximate rate of IDR 16,000 = USD 1.

Legend used throughout: **[DEDICATED]** = product sold specifically as speaker-cabinet texture paint; **[REPURPOSED]** = generic building/automotive/industrial coating that would be repurposed.

## Key question 1: Which Indonesian products are the closest equivalents to Warnex / Tuff Cab (1K water-based acrylic texture paint), and what do they cost per kg/litre?

### Takeaway
Indonesia has its own category of cheap, locally made "cat texture box speaker" sold in 1 kg / 1 L cans (brands Vircan, Caldera, Prismatik, Dexa) for roughly IDR 75,000–100,000 per kg (USD 5–6), i.e. about one-fifth of the Warnex/Armacab price per kg; they are almost always sold only in black ("hitam"), and none of the sources found states the resin chemistry or a grey option. Generic water-based acrylic "kamprot"/texture wall paints (Avian Avitex Tekstur TX-100, Norden Texturoll, Propan Decor DTX-250/280, Tekskam, Boxer) are the next-closest substitute and are widely stocked in 1–30 kg packs.

### Inferences
- Per-kg cost comparison (approx.): Indonesian Vircan/Caldera/Prismatik IDR 75,000–100,000/kg (USD 5–6); Armacab UK £17.87/L (about IDR 360,000 / USD 22); DuraTex USD 96.98/gal (about USD 26/L); Warnex via Thomann (price not retrievable here, historically in the EUR 20–25/kg range). The local products are 4–5x cheaper before any shipping.
- The local Indonesian speaker texture paints are marketed with the words "anti air" and "lebih bagus dari resin" and the Indana text says they are applied undiluted and textured with a brush/roller, which is consistent with a 1K water-based acrylic emulsion like Warnex/Tuff Cab/Armacab; however no source actually states the binder, hardness, or scrub/scratch rating, so durability parity with Warnex/Armacab is unproven.
- All found dedicated Indonesian speaker paints are black ("hitam"/"jet black"). No grey variant was found in any listing. Obtaining dark grey would require (a) asking the seller/maker for a custom colour, (b) tinting a white/base texture paint at a paint shop, or (c) using a generic grey kamprot texture (Tekskam light grey exists, but light not dark) and over-coating.
- Generic kamprot/texture wall paints (Avitex TX-100, Norden, Propan DTX) are sand/filler-loaded wall textures; their texture is coarser and the film softer than a cabinet-grade product, so they should be regarded as a last-resort base rather than an equivalent.

### Gaps
- Manufacturer identity and TDS for Vircan, Caldera, Prismatik and Dexa (resin type, solids, hardness, VOC, drying time, coverage) could not be found; the Indana article (the only manufacturer-side page) could not be opened. Whether "Vircan" is an Indana brand is unconfirmed.
- No Indonesian listing in dark grey for any dedicated speaker texture paint was found.
- No Kaskus forum thread on "cat box speaker tekstur" surfaced in search; Indonesian discussion appears to live on YouTube and TikTok Shop instead.
- Live Tokopedia/Shopee/BigGo pages could not be opened, so sold counts, seller locations and current stock are from excerpts only.

## Key question 2: Can any imported speaker paint realistically be bought in Indonesia (shipping cost, lead time, who sells it)?

### Takeaway
No Indonesian distributor, dealer or marketplace listing for Warnex, Tuff Cab, DuraTex, Armacab or HIFIDECO CY-689 was found; the realistic routes are (1) asking Adam Hall's exclusive Indonesian distributor SVI (Suara Visual Indonesia) to special-order Warnex, (2) asking Penn Elcom's Kuala Lumpur hub (Penn Elcom Sdn. Bhd., Puchong) to ship Armacab/Warnex to Indonesia, or (3) parcel-forwarding from Thomann/Parts Express, with Indonesian import duty (7.5% on consignments over USD 3) plus VAT on top.

### Inferences
- Because Warnex is explicitly "not dangerous goods" and water-based, it is the easiest imported product to bring in via courier; solvent-borne or aerosol alternatives would face carrier restrictions. The practical cost stack for a 1 kg can is: product (~EUR 20–25) + international shipping (likely EUR 30–60 for a 1.3 kg parcel) + 7.5% duty + 11% VAT, i.e. roughly IDR 1–1.5 million landed per kg versus IDR 75–100k for a local product, so imports only make sense for small batches or reference samples.
- SVI already carries Adam Hall's "Hardware" catalogue line through its distributorship; a special order of Warnex (Adam Hall item 0131/74.91.004.3 family) through SVI is the most realistic official channel, but no evidence was found that SVI stocks it.
- Penn Elcom KL is the nearest Armacab stock point; Penn Elcom's distributor page invites direct contact for unlisted countries.

### Gaps
- Thomann's Indonesia-specific shipping rate and transit time page was not retrievable.
- No evidence that SVI, PT Citra Intirama or any Indonesian pro-audio dealer has ever stocked Warnex, Armacab or Tuff Cab.
- Blue Aran's delivery page and Acry-Tech's international shipping policy could not be opened; no confirmation that either ships to Indonesia.
- Current Indonesian de minimis/duty rules (post-2023 PMK revisions) not verified against the primary regulation.

## Key question 3: Which 2K PU or polyurea options exist locally for a tougher Funktion-One-style finish, and what do applicators charge?

### Takeaway
Indonesia has ample locally stocked 2K aliphatic polyurethane topcoats (Jotun Hardtop XP/AX in RAL colours at IDR ~1.0–1.25 million per 5 L set, Propan PU/Durogloss/Duratex lines, Nippon PU, Kansai Paralux) and a growing base of spray-polyurea waterproofing contractors in Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, Semarang and Yogyakarta, but no Line-X, Rhino Linings dealer, U-POL Raptor retailer, or polyurea applicator with any speaker-cabinet or small-object experience was found, and no published IDR price per m² for polyurea application could be verified.

## Key question 5: Indonesian climate considerations (RH 70–90%, 28–34 °C) for curing water-based and 2K coatings; what local TDS say

### Takeaway
Local and international TDS converge on the same limits: water-based coatings need RH below about 80–85% (Propan specifies max 80% RH and 25–35 °C; Teknos says drying slows above 85% RH), and Jotun Hardtop XP requires 10–85% RH, substrate at least 3 °C above dew point, and no exposure above 85% RH before walk-on-dry or blushing/gloss loss occurs; Indonesian mornings and wet-season days routinely exceed these, so dehumidified/ventilated indoor curing and avoiding early-morning or rainy-day application are required. The Blue Aran Tuff Cab ceiling of 25 °C is unrealistic in Indonesia and implies faster open-time management.

### Cited Findings
- Propan Raya (Indonesian) acrylic roof-paint TDS: application at maximum 80% RH, 25–35 °C, with adequate ventilation during application and drying. — [Propan Cat Genteng TDS](https://propanraya.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/PROPAN-CAT-GENTENG.pdf)
- Propan DECOR Acrylic Enamel DAE 560 WB and DURATEX DRX 890 WB TDS exist (water-based acrylic / 2K WB PU) with Indonesian application guidance. — [Propan DAE 560 WB TDS](https://www.propanraya.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/DECOR-Acrylic-Enamel-DAE-560-WB-1.pdf); [Propan DRX 890 WB TDS](https://www.propanraya.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/DURATEX-2K-PU-DRX-890-WB.pdf)

- Blue Aran Tuff Cab: apply at room temperature, not below 12 °C or above 25 °C. — [Blue Aran blog](https://blog.bluearan.co.uk/?p=88)
- Warnex SDS: store between 15 °C and 30 °C in well-ventilated dry rooms. — [Warnex SDS](https://adamhall.s3.amazonaws.com/media/MARKEN/WARNEX/0131WHI/0131WHI_WARNEX%20Strukturlack_Technisches_Datenblatt_de.pdf)
- Avitex Tekstur TX-100 dry time 1–2 h (Indonesian product, presumably at Indonesian conditions). — [Avian TX-100](https://en.avianbrands.com/produk/avitex-tekstur-tx100)

### Inferences
- At 30 °C and 85% RH the dew point is about 27 °C, so a plywood cabinet stored indoors at 28 °C sits barely 1 °C above dew point in the morning; Jotun's 3 °C rule is therefore a real constraint and argues for spraying 2K PU in the warmer, drier afternoon or in an air-conditioned/dehumidified room.
- Water-based texture paints will skin fast at 30–34 °C but through-dry slowly at high RH; thin coats, fans with exhaust, and longer recoat windows (Propan-style "max 80% RH") are the local practice signalled by TDS.
- Imported water-based products (Warnex storage 15–30 °C; Tuff Cab ruined below 5 °C) will not be harmed by Indonesian ambient storage temperatures but containers should be kept out of direct sun above 30 °C.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian-language TDS for the dedicated Vircan/Caldera/Prismatik speaker paints, so their recommended RH/temperature window is unknown.
- Jotun Indonesia's local TDS (as opposed to the global English TDS) was not retrievable.

## Key question 6: Indonesian health & safety and availability constraints for isocyanate 2K products (respirators, booths)
